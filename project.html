Ember Chair

2025

The Ember Chair explores the boundary between structural honesty and warmth. Bent steel meets hand-finished ash wood in a form that invites extended sitting without sacrificing its visual lightness. Manufactured in a single small-batch run of 40 units, each chair is individually numbered.
