About

Shaping ideas into form.

I’m Arsalan, an Industrial Designer who finds joy in the details. My work is a quiet exploration of form, function, and the technicalities of manufacturing. I spend my days navigating the space between digital pixels and physical materials, trying to create products that feel ‘just right.’ I value precision, honest materials, and the continuous journey of learning. I’m here to contribute, to help build better solutions, and to share what I’ve learned with the design community.
